## Pricing benchmark references

Prices in the demo catalog are realistic U.S.-market-style menu prices, informed by current bakery pricing references available in September 2026, including:

- Crumbl large desserts: single $4.49; 4-pack $15.99; 6-pack $22.99; 12-pack $42.99 in a sampled U.S. location.
- Nothing Bundt Cakes: standard cakes shown starting around $33.52 and decorated cakes around $43–$45 in sampled locations; location-specific prices vary.

The prices shown on martineades-RR are not copied menus or resale prices from those brands. They are a demo catalog benchmark and should be validated for your actual ingredients, labor, market and delivery costs before accepting orders.
