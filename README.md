# 🎂 martineades-RR

A polished static storefront for a cake, cookie and celebration-dessert business.

## Included

- 50 bakery menu items across 7 categories
- Cakes, wedding cakes, cupcakes, cookies, brownies, cheesecakes, tarts, donuts and macarons
- Responsive product grid
- Category filters, search and price sorting
- Functional front-end shopping cart
- Custom cake consultation form
- Support and cake-consultation contact areas
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile
- Real bakery/dessert photography references from free Unsplash images
- Boutique pastel visual identity using the martineades-RR logo

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The product photos are loaded from Unsplash URLs, so an internet connection is required for those photos.

## Important before publishing

- Replace the demo address with the verified business address.
- Replace the `555` phone numbers with your real support/consultation numbers.
- Connect checkout to your preferred payment provider.
- Confirm final product pricing, availability and delivery fees for your real market.

## Pricing note

The menu uses realistic U.S. bakery price benchmarks researched in September 2026. Custom cakes and local bakery pricing vary by size, design and location, so update prices before taking real orders.
